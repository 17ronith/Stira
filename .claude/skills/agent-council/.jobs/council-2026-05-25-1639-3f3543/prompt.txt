Stira is a local-first Windows desktop application (Mac to follow). When you sit down to work, you tell Stira what you intend to do. A lightweight, three-layer intent engine — running entirely on your machine — interprets that expression of intent, moving from fast local classifiers up to a Claude API fallback for edge cases it can't resolve locally. This pipeline figures out not just what you said, but what you meant, and uses that to reshape your digital environment accordingly. Enforcement happens through a dedicated agent layer — currently being decided between NemoClaw, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, and Open Human — which monitors your session and holds you to the intent you declared, making it meaningfully harder to drift into distraction mid-session. Business model: One-time purchase with an optional subscription, a 14-day free trial (no card required), and a permanent free tier. Pricing is PPP-adjusted via Paddle, and go-to-market is bootstrapped with paid spend split between Reddit and Instagram.
